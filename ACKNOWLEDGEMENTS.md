# Acknowledgements

## Stay Awake Design Language & Minute Tape Scrubber

Wipextension adapts the minute tape scrubber design language and velocity model from **Stay Awake**, developed by **Jordy Spruit** (creator of Droppy), with permission.

- **Author:** Jordy Spruit
- **App:** Droppy (Stay Awake / Pomodoro)
- **Website:** [https://getdroppy.app](https://getdroppy.app)
- **Reference Kit:** Stay Awake Design Reuse Kit (October 13, 2026)

The minute ruler mechanics, Canvas rendering techniques, haptic detent feedback integration, scrub energy bloom model, and adaptive typography were adapted from Jordy's Stay Awake implementation for Droppy.
