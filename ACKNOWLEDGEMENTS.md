# Acknowledgements

## High Alert Design Language & Minute Tape Scrubber

Wipextension adapts the minute tape scrubber design language and velocity model from **High Alert**, developed by **Jordy Spruit** (creator of Droppy), with permission.

- **Author:** Jordy Spruit
- **App:** Droppy (High Alert / Pomodoro)
- **Website:** [https://getdroppy.app](https://getdroppy.app)
- **Reference Kit:** High Alert Design Reuse Kit (October 13, 2026)

The minute ruler mechanics, Canvas rendering techniques, haptic detent feedback integration, scrub energy bloom model, and adaptive typography were adapted from Jordy's High Alert implementation for Droppy.
